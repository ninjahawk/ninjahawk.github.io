Exhibit 01 · Subtext

What a model holds before it speaks

This is my interpretability tool Subtext, running a real recorded session. It applies a Jacobian activation lens at nine depths inside a small LLM (Qwen3.5-4B) and decodes the hidden states through the vocabulary — amber while the model reads, blue while it replies. Watch the verdict form several tokens before anything is said. Hover any word for its layer trace.

open full-screen ↗ source · ★183

Honest caveat: an activation lens shows what the residual stream is disposed to say, not ground-truth "thoughts." Decodings can be noisy, and this is a 4B model — treat it as a microscope with a known distortion, documented in the repo.

Exhibit 02 · Singleton Attractors (preprint)

Tune the singularity

My preprint asks: if several AI agents recursively self-improve while competing for shared resources, does one inevitably dominate? This is the paper's actual ODE system — dSi/dt = Si1−β(Si) · Siα/ΣSjα — integrated live for three agents. Drag the sliders. Find the regime where the leader escapes to infinity in finite time.

● 2026 calibration: estimated β stays > 0 — the real world is (so far) not in the divergent regime
read the preprint (PDF) ↗ proofs + 11 simulations · repo

Honest caveat: the theorems are conditional. Calibration against Epoch AI compute and benchmark data through 2026 does not support assumption A4 — this playground shows what the math says would happen if the feedback exponent ever flipped. Coalition results (critical strength α* ≈ 0.64) are in the paper.

Agents left alone
