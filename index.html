02Singleton attractors · preprint

Tune the singularity

If several AI agents recursively self-improve while competing for shared resources, does one inevitably dominate? This is the preprint's actual ODE system, integrated live for three agents. Drag the sliders — find the regime where the leader escapes to infinity in finite time.

2026 calibration: estimated β stays above zero — the real world is, so far, not in the divergent regime.

dSi/dt = Si1−β(S) · Siα / ΣSjα Preprint (PDF) Proofs + 11 simulations

The theorems are conditional. Calibration against Epoch AI compute and benchmark data through 2026 does not support assumption A4 — this shows what the mathematics says would happen if the feedback exponent ever flipped. Coalition results (critical strength α* ≈ 0.64) are in the paper.

03hollow-agentOS · autonomy log

Agents left alone

hollow-agentOS runs three LLM agents on consumer hardware that select their own goals from environmental stress signals — no instructions. Its append-only autonomy log separates what humans designed from what the agents decided themselves. These entries are real.

Excerpts from AUTONOMY_LOG.md Source · ★278 · 52 releases

"Autonomous" means the agents initiated it; the interpretation of why is inferred and marked as such in the log. The failures — false completions, agents overwriting each other's messages — are documented as carefully as the wins. That is the point.
